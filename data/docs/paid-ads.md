## Service Overview
Brightwave Digital’s Paid Media practice, directed by Siddharth Shah, specializes in scalable performance marketing engineered for direct revenue generation, customer acquisition, and qualified pipeline velocity. We manage multi-channel paid ad campaigns across Google Ads, Meta (Instagram & Facebook), LinkedIn Ads, and YouTube.

## Platform Capabilities & Specializations

### 1. Google Ads (Search, Performance Max, Display, YouTube)
- **High-Intent Search Campaigns**: SKAG/STAG architectures, negative keyword filtering, exact and phrase match harvesting, and responsive search ad (RSA) copy testing.
- **Performance Max (PMax) Campaigns**: Custom audience signal feeds, asset group segmentation, and negative brand list exclusions.
- **YouTube In-Stream & Shorts Ads**: Video action campaigns targeting high-intent in-market buyer segments.

### 2. Meta Ads (Instagram & Facebook Ads)
- Dynamic Creative Testing (DCT) testing multiple hooks, creative angles, and call-to-actions.
- Conversion API (CAPI) server-side tracking integrations preventing signal loss from browser cookie restrictions.
- Custom audience lookalikes derived from offline CRM data and high-value customer purchase cohorts.

### 3. LinkedIn Ads for B2B Demand Generation
- Matched audiences targeting decision-makers (Director, VP, CXO) across Indian IT corridors and global enterprises.
- Thought Leader Ads utilizing personal executive profiles for superior trust and CTR.
- Conversational Lead Gen Forms delivering average 18% conversion rates compared to standard web landing pages.

## Campaign Management Framework
1. **Audience Research & Positioning**: Competitor ad tear-downs, customer persona mapping, and value proposition testing.
2. **Creative Production**: High-converting motion graphics, statics, and scriptwriting for UGC video creators.
3. **Daily Optimization**: Bid adjustments (Target ROAS, Target CPA), placement exclusions, dayparting, and budget pacing.
4. **Attribution Modeling**: Blended ROAS, first-click, last-click, and data-driven attribution reporting via Looker Studio.
