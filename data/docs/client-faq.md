## General Client Questions

### Where is Brightwave Digital located?
Our primary delivery headquarters is located at 6th Floor, Synergy Tower, SG Highway, Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380054, India. We also maintain satellite client consultation desks in Mumbai (BKC) and Bengaluru (Indiranagar).

### How quickly can a new campaign launch after signing?
Standard onboarding requires 10 business days from contract execution and initial payment receipt. During this window, we complete technical audits, configure tracking pixels, establish 1Password guest vaults, and align on initial creative deliverables. Live campaign activation commences on Day 11.

### Who owns the ad accounts and creative assets?
The client retains 100% legal ownership of all advertising accounts (Google Ads, Meta Business Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager), custom creative assets, Figma design files, and Looker Studio dashboards. Brightwave Digital operates strictly with Agency Partner access. If our partnership terminates, you retain uninterrupted access to all accounts and historical performance data.

### What are your standard business and support hours?
Our agency operations operate Monday through Friday from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST. Dedicated Slack Connect channels are monitored continuously during these hours. Urgent campaign disruptions or production outages outside business hours are handled 24/7 through our automated emergency escalation matrix.

### What is the minimum contract commitment?
Our Silver Growth package requires a minimum 3-month commitment to allow sufficient time for search algorithm indexing and algorithmic ad learning. Our Gold Scale and Platinum Market Leader packages carry a 6-month commitment. After the initial term, retainers transition to convenient month-to-month contracts subject to a 30-day written cancellation notice.

### Does your retainer fee include advertising media spend?
No. All ad spend is paid directly by your company to Google, Meta, or LinkedIn using your corporate credit card or direct credit line. Our retainer fee covers strategic planning, copywriting, design, technical optimization, ongoing management, and reporting.
