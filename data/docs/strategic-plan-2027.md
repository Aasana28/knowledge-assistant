## Strict Confidentiality Notice
This strategic planning dossier contains proprietary executive projections, capital allocation decisions, headcount growth targets, and acquisition roadmaps for Brightwave Digital. Access is strictly limited to Managing Partners and Board Directors. Unauthorized sharing constitutes a material breach of fiduciary duty.

## Three-Year Revenue Targets and Financial Milestones
Managing Partner Vikram Joshi, in consultation with Finance Head Manish Gokhale, has formalized the three-year financial trajectory:
1. **FY2026 Performance Baseline**: Closing at ₹26.00 Crore in annual recurring agency revenue with 24% net operating profit margin.
2. **FY2027 Revenue Target**: Brightwave has established a formal revenue target of ₹48,00,00,000 (₹48.00 Crore ARR) for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027. This represents an 84.6% top-line growth trajectory driven by enterprise retainers.
3. **FY2028 Aspirational Benchmark**: Reaching ₹75.00 Crore ARR and exploring a domestic IPO or pre-IPO institutional round on the NSE Emerge platform.

## Planned Talent Headcount Expansion
To sustain this growth velocity, headcount will scale systematically across our Ahmedabad engineering base and regional offices:
- Current full-time employee count stands at 45 team members as of September 2026.
- The company will expand headcount to 95 full-time employees by December 2027.
- Priority talent acquisitions include:
  - 12 Senior Performance Media Buyers
  - 8 AI/ML Engineers specializing in marketing automation
  - 15 Senior Content Strategists and Creative Directors
  - 2 Regional Sales Vice Presidents stationed in Dubai and Singapore.

## Strategic M&A (Mergers and Acquisitions) Roadmap
Brightwave has earmarked a capital war chest of ₹10,00,00,000 (₹10.00 Crore) for strategic acquisitions over the next 18 months:
1. **Project Nectar (Pune)**: Target acquisition of a boutique Generative AI and synthetic video studio in Pune with an approved deal valuation cap of ₹4,50,00,000 (₹4.50 Crore). Target closing date: Q1 2027.
2. **Project Falcon (Bengaluru)**: Acquisition of a high-growth Amazon and quick-commerce performance agency in Indiranagar, Bengaluru, budgeted at ₹3,50,00,000 (₹3.50 Crore), planned for Q3 2027.

## Geographic Footprint Expansion
- Brightwave will inaugurate its first international liaison office in Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), United Arab Emirates, by May 2027 (Q2 2027). This hub will target luxury real estate and regional fintech retainers across the GCC.
