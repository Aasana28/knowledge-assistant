## Confidentiality & Commercial Purpose
This policy document establishes mandatory commercial thresholds, gross margin guardrails, and hierarchical discount approval limits for all client contracts generated by Brightwave Digital. This document is strictly restricted to Finance and Executive Management.

## Absolute Margin Guardrails
1. **Target Gross Margin**: All standard proposals and retainer pricing models are engineered to achieve a target gross margin of 55%.
2. **Hard Minimum Gross Margin**: Under no operational or competitive circumstance may any client engagement be signed with a projected gross margin below 42%. Proposals failing this floor are automatically vetoed by Finance.
3. **Minimum Contract Value Floor**: Even with approved discounts, no monthly retainer may be booked below the baseline floor of ₹60,000 per month (+ GST).

## Hierarchical Discount Authority Limits
Discounts on Brightwave Digital’s standard retainer rates (Silver ₹75,000, Gold ₹1,50,000, Platinum ₹2,75,000) are governed strictly by the following approval thresholds:

- **Tier 1 Discount (0.0% to 5.0%)**:
  - *Authority*: Account Directors or VP of Client Success.
  - *Condition*: Applicable solely for clients committing to a 12-month non-cancellable contract with full 100% upfront annual payment.
- **Tier 2 Discount (5.1% to 10.0%)**:
  - *Authority*: Manish Gokhale, Head of Finance.
  - *Condition*: Requires written financial justification submitted via Jira Finance ticket, verifying client credit rating and minimum estimated spend volume.
- **Tier 3 Discount (10.1% to 15.0%)**:
  - *Authority*: Unanimous joint written sign-off from both Manish Gokhale (Head of Finance) and Vikram Joshi (Managing Partner).
  - *Condition*: Reserved exclusively for multi-year enterprise accounts or strategic logo acquisition.
- **Hard Maximum Ceiling**:
  - **The absolute maximum discount percentage permitted across Brightwave Digital is 15.0%**. No employee, director, or partner possesses authority to quote or contract discounts exceeding 15%.

## Ad Spend Commission Discretion
For ad spend exceeding ₹15,00,000 per month, standard management fees transition from fixed retainer to 8% of gross ad spend. Finance may negotiate this down to 6.5% for media budgets exceeding ₹50,00,000 monthly, provided the 42% minimum margin test is satisfied.
