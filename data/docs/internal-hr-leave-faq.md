## Employee Welfare and Attendance Standards
This document provides key answers regarding human resources policies, leave entitlement, remote work regulations, and health benefits at Brightwave Digital. Rashmi Patel leads People Operations.

## Leave Entitlement and Quotas
Brightwave Digital provides 24 days of paid time off per calendar year (January 1 to December 31) for confirmed full-time team members, credited on a pro-rata basis:
- **Privilege Leaves (PL)**: 12 days per annum. Up to 6 unused PL days can be carried forward to the following calendar year, with a lifetime accumulation cap of 30 days. Must be applied at least 7 days in advance via Darwinbox.
- **Casual Leaves (CL)**: 8 days per annum. Designed for personal exigencies. Cannot be clubbed with more than 2 consecutive days.
- **Sick Leaves (SL)**: 4 days per annum. Absences exceeding 2 consecutive days require a signed medical certificate from a registered practitioner.
- **Maternity & Paternity Benefits**: 26 weeks paid maternity leave in accordance with statutory norms; 2 weeks (10 business days) paid paternity leave.

## Hybrid Work and Office Hours
1. **Hybrid Schedule**: Brightwave operates on a 3:2 hybrid schedule. Employees work from the Ahmedabad headquarters on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated flexible remote work days.
2. **Core Hours**: All employees are expected to be available online on Slack and for internal meetings between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM IST. Standard agency shift spans 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST.

## Healthcare & Wellness Coverage
- Every full-time employee and their immediate dependents (spouse and up to two children) are enrolled in a ₹5,00,000 cashless group health insurance policy underwritten by Star Health Insurance.
- Annual health wellness checkups are hosted every November at our Ahmedabad office campus in partnership with Apollo Hospitals.

## Notice Periods and Separation
- Confirmed full-time employees have a formal notice period of 60 calendar days.
- Team members on initial 3-month probation have a 30-day notice period.
- Buyout of notice period requires explicit joint approval from Rashmi Patel and Managing Partner Vikram Joshi.
