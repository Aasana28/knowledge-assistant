## Purpose and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs the formal intake and onboarding sequence for all new retainers and project clients at Brightwave Digital, Ahmedabad. Adherence ensures seamless technical transitions, zero credential loss, and SLA alignment within 10 business days of contract execution.

## Phase 1: Handover and Contract Verification (Days 1–2)
1. Upon contract sign-off in DocuSign, the Business Development team transfers account ownership to Neha Parikh (Head of Client Success) via internal Slack channel `#client-handovers`.
2. The assigned Account Manager verifies that the initial retainer deposit (50% upfront for projects or 100% first month for retainers) is credited to Brightwave's HDFC Bank current account.
3. Account Manager initializes the client workspace in ClickUp using the `Standard-Agency-Onboarding-v4` template.

## Phase 2: Kickoff Meeting and Asset Collection (Days 3–5)
1. The Account Manager schedules a 60-minute virtual or in-person kickoff meeting at our Ahmedabad headquarters on SG Highway.
2. The client is provided access to a 1Password guest vault containing invitations to share Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Search Console, Google Ads Manager, and Meta Business Suite credentials. Under no circumstances should passwords be transmitted via plain email or WhatsApp.
3. The Strategy Lead completes the Brand Questionnaire covering brand guidelines, tone of voice, top three direct competitors, and target demographics across Tier 1 and Tier 2 Indian cities.

## Phase 3: Technical Audit and Roadmap Sign-off (Days 6–10)
1. Technical and SEO specialists conduct a baseline audit within 72 hours of receiving asset access.
2. The Account Manager delivers the 90-Day Performance Roadmap outlining milestones, KPIs, weekly sync slots (Tuesdays or Thursdays between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM IST), and reporting cadence.
3. Onboarding formally concludes when the client approves the initial sprint backlog in ClickUp and signs the Onboarding Acceptance Certificate.
