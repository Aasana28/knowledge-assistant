## Service Overview
Brightwave Digital delivers enterprise-grade Search Engine Optimization (SEO) engineered to build sustainable, high-intent organic traffic for B2B enterprises, D2C brands, and multi-location businesses across India and international markets. Kunal Trivedi leads our SEO Practice Group from our Ahmedabad studio.

## Core Pillars of our SEO Delivery

### 1. Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals Optimization
We conduct forensic audits inspecting indexability, crawl budget allocation, XML sitemaps, robots.txt directives, canonicalization, JavaScript rendering issues, and structured schema markup (JSON-LD for Organization, Product, FAQPage, and LocalBusiness). We optimize site architecture to achieve 90+ mobile Google PageSpeed scores, resolving Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) under 2.5s, Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) under 0.1, and Interaction to Next Paint (INP) under 200ms.

### 2. On-Page & Semantic Content Architecture
Our team maps target keyword clusters using Semrush, Ahrefs, and Google Keyword Planner. We construct semantic topical authority clusters around commercial and informational search intents. Every landing page receives customized metadata, semantic H1–H4 header hierarchy, keyword-balanced internal linking, and conversion-focused copy.

### 3. High-Authority Link Building & Digital PR
Brightwave adheres strictly to white-hat link acquisition standards. We execute proactive digital PR outreach, guest thought leadership contributions on authoritative industry publications (Domain Rating 50+), broken backlink reclamation, and regional brand citations across verified Indian business directories.

### 4. Local SEO for Multi-Location Businesses
For multi-city brands, we optimize Google Business Profiles (GBP), harmonize Name-Address-Phone (NAP) consistency across 50+ aggregators, implement localized geo-targeted schema, and configure review collection funnels.

## Typical Deliverables & Timeline
- Month 1: 40-point Technical SEO Audit, keyword research matrix, initial quick-win on-page fixes.
- Month 2: Content cluster creation, internal linking graph re-engineering, initial 5 link placements.
- Month 3 onwards: Bi-weekly technical crawls, recurring high-intent blog publications, monthly ranking tracking across desktop and mobile SERPs.
