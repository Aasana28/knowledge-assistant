## Overview
Brightwave Digital maintains rigorous reporting governance to ensure transparent ROI demonstration across all digital marketing campaigns. Rohan Mehta oversees cross-team reporting compliance. Reports are categorized into Weekly Flash Updates, Monthly Executive Reviews, and Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs).

## Weekly Flash Updates
- **Audience & Delivery**: Direct client POC via automated email digest and Slack Connect channel.
- **Delivery Timeline**: Every Monday by 11:30 AM IST.
- **Key Metrics Included**: 7-day spend in INR, total conversions/leads generated, average Cost Per Lead (CPL) or Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) for eCommerce accounts, and active sprint deliverables.
- **Responsible Party**: Assigned Account Executive drafts data from Looker Studio; Account Manager reviews and sends.

## Monthly Executive Performance Reports
- **Audience & Delivery**: C-Suite, VP Marketing, and agency stakeholders via interactive Looker Studio dashboards accompanied by a comprehensive 12-page PDF slide deck.
- **Delivery Timeline**: Generated on the 3rd business day of each month and dispatched by the 5th business day by 5:00 PM IST.
- **Key Components**: Organic traffic trends, keyword rankings (top 3, top 10 positions), paid media blended efficiency, social media engagement rates, completed deliverables, budget reconciliation, and month-ahead optimization focus.
- **Review Meeting**: A mandatory 45-minute review call is hosted between the 6th and 10th of every month.

## Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)
- **Schedule**: First two weeks following the close of Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4 (fiscal year ending March 31).
- **Structure**: Comprehensive multi-channel attribution analysis, competitive market share benchmarks, industry trend forecasting, and contract renewal or expansion recommendations.
- **Sign-off Requirement**: Every QBR presentation must be co-reviewed by Managing Partner Vikram Joshi 48 hours prior to the client presentation.
