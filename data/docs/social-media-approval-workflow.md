## Objective
This SOP standardizes social media content creation, internal peer review, client sign-off, and scheduling across Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, and X for all agency accounts managed by Brightwave Digital. Pooja Shah (Creative Operations Director) serves as primary process owner.

## Step 1: Monthly Content Calendar Drafting (Days 15–20 of Month T-1)
1. The Content Strategist reviews client theme briefs, upcoming product launches, Indian festive dates (e.g., Diwali, Uttarayan, Navratri), and trending audio/topics.
2. An editorial grid of 12 to 20 post concepts is prepared in Planoly / Notion, specifying visual asset type (static carousel, motion graphic, 9:16 reel), copy in English or Gujarati, hashtags, and CTA.
3. Graphic designers complete draft creative assets in Figma by the 20th of the month.

## Step 2: Internal Quality Gate (Days 21–23)
1. Copy is proofread by the Senior Content Editor for factual accuracy, spelling, and tone alignment.
2. Pooja Shah inspects visual assets against client brand guidelines (pantone color codes, font usage, logo safe zones).
3. Any revisions must be returned to the design team with 24 hours turnaround.

## Step 3: Client Review and Sign-Off (Days 24–28)
1. The calendar is submitted through the Brightwave Client Review Portal or Notion board by 5:00 PM IST on the 24th.
2. Clients are asked to review, leave comments, and approve items within 3 business days.
3. If no client response is received after 48 hours, the Account Manager triggers a reminder call.

## Step 4: Scheduling and Real-time Engagement
1. All approved posts are scheduled in Sprout Social by 6:00 PM IST on the final business day of the month.
2. For real-time topical posts or moment marketing, an expedited 4-hour approval protocol is followed: draft shared via emergency Slack ping to client POC, requiring one explicit written confirmation before posting.
