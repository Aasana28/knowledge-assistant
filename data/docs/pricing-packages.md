## Overview of Brightwave Retainer Tiers
Brightwave Digital offers three standardized monthly retainer packages tailored to growing startups, mid-market enterprises, and national brands. All pricing is stated in Indian Rupees (INR) exclusive of applicable 18% Goods and Services Tax (GST). Managed media spend is paid directly by clients to advertising platforms and is separate from agency service retainers.

## Retainer Tiers & Inclusions

### 1. Silver Growth Retainer (₹75,000 / month + GST)
- **Target Audience**: Early-stage companies and specialized local businesses establishing predictable digital acquisition.
- **Included Scope**:
  - Full Technical SEO baseline audit plus on-page optimization for up to 15 core landing pages.
  - 4 high-authority, search-optimized blog posts per month (1,200 words each).
  - Single-channel Paid Media management (Google Ads or Meta Ads) managing up to ₹2,50,000 in monthly ad spend.
  - Monthly automated Looker Studio executive dashboard.
  - Bi-weekly 30-minute progress calls with an Account Executive.
- **Contract Term**: Minimum 3-month initial commitment, transitioning to 30-day rolling cancellation.

### 2. Gold Scale Retainer (₹1,50,000 / month + GST)
- **Target Audience**: Established mid-market companies scaling customer acquisition across multiple channels.
- **Included Scope**:
  - Comprehensive SEO architecture covering up to 40 landing pages plus continuous competitor keyword monitoring.
  - 8 long-form content assets per month (1,500 words each) and active digital PR link outreach (5 backlinks/month).
  - Multi-channel Paid Media management (Google Ads + Meta Ads + LinkedIn Ads) managing up to ₹7,50,000 monthly ad spend.
  - Social media creative calendar: 12 designed posts/reels per month with community moderation.
  - Weekly flash reporting email plus dedicated monthly review call.
  - Dedicated Account Manager with guaranteed 4-hour business day communication turnaround.
- **Contract Term**: Minimum 6-month initial engagement.

### 3. Platinum Market Leader Retainer (₹2,75,000 / month + GST)
- **Target Audience**: High-growth category leaders, D2C conglomerates, and global B2B enterprises requiring full-funnel digital dominance.
- **Included Scope**:
  - Uncapped search optimization covering unlimited landing pages, international SEO, and ongoing technical maintenance.
  - 16 authority articles per month, 2 quarterly whitepapers/case studies, and executive thought leadership ghostwriting.
  - Multi-channel performance marketing with uncapped monthly media spend across Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and YouTube.
  - Creative suite: 20 bespoke static graphics, 8 edited short-form video reels, and conversion copy testing.
  - Server-Side GTM, GA4 BigQuery export, and conversion rate optimization (CRO) testing.
  - Direct Slack Connect channel, priority 1-hour SLA, and monthly executive consultation with Managing Partner Vikram Joshi.
- **Contract Term**: Minimum 6-month initial engagement.
