## Scope and Financial Governance
This document specifies the billing cycles, tax documentation protocols, credit periods, late penalty structures, and default enforcement procedures enforced by the Brightwave Digital Finance Department under Manish Gokhale.

## Recurring Billing Cycle & Invoicing Dates
1. **Advance Billing Schedule**: All monthly retainer agreements are billed in advance. Invoices are automatically generated in Zoho Books on the 25th day of every calendar month for the subsequent service month (e.g., invoices generated on October 25th cover November 1–30 service).
2. **Project Milestones**: Fixed-scope development or audit projects are invoiced on a 50-30-20 milestone structure (50% mobilization advance upon signing, 30% upon staging delivery, 20% upon final production sign-off).
3. **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**: All tax invoices include 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST for clients within Gujarat, or 18% IGST for interstate clients). HSN/SAC Code for digital marketing services is 998311.

## Credit Terms and Payment Due Dates
- **Standard Payment Terms**: Net 15 days from the date of invoice issuance. Consequently, monthly retainer payments are due on the 10th day of the service month.
- **Accepted Modes**: RTGS, NEFT, or IMPS into Brightwave Digital’s corporate bank account at HDFC Bank (Navrangpura Branch, Ahmedabad; IFSC: HDFC0000006). International remittances must arrive via wire in USD or EUR to our designated EEFC account.
- **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)**: Clients deducting TDS under Section 194C (2% for advertising contracts) or Section 194J (10% for professional technical services) must upload formal Form 16A quarterly TDS certificates to the finance portal within 45 days of quarter end.

## Penalties and Service Suspension Protocols
1. **Late Payment Fee**: Accounts outstanding beyond Net 15 days incur a mandatory late payment penalty calculated at 1.5% per month (18% annualized) compounded monthly on the total unpaid overdue balance.
2. **Grace Period & Reminder Protocol**: Polite automated reminders are dispatched on Day +5 and Day +10 past due.
3. **Work Suspension Trigger**: If any invoice remains unpaid after 30 calendar days from issuance, the Finance Lead enforces automatic project freeze: all live ad campaigns across Google and Meta are paused, deliverables are halted, and client portal access is revoked until full account clearance.
